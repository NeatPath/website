About us

Built for this decade, not the nineties

Cloud-native voice and core networks, built by a team with around 20 years of voice and IMS domain expertise. Everything we ship is built in-house in Golang: cloud-native, zero legacy.

How we think

DESIGNED FOR THIS DECADE
  • Old cores, new requirements — an IMS designed 20 years ago cannot fulfil today's network, deployment and operations requirements; ours does.
  • Cloud-native — deployed on your cloud or ours, scaled by software, not hardware refreshes.
  • Modern engineering — Golang microservices, memory-safe and observable by construction.
LEAN BY DESIGN
  • Fewer moving parts — faster integration, lower OPEX, simpler upgrades.
  • No legacy stacks — nothing rewrapped, nothing carried forward.
BUILT FROM SCRATCH
  • Every product in-house — from the IMS core and ePDG to mobile core, HSS and SMS functions.
  • Proven — tested with real handsets and integrated with other vendors' cores, HSS and IMS functions, including Tier 1 suppliers.
