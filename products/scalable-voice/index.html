THE GAP IT CLOSES

Carrier-grade voice without legacy weight

  • The problem with "proven" cores — an IMS designed 20 years ago cannot fulfil today's network, deployment and operations requirements. Ours is designed for exactly those.
  • Full IMS core — P-CSCF, S-CSCF and I-CSCF, with the latest encryption algorithms and Release 19 alignment.
  • Emergency and messaging ready — E-CSCF / IP-SM-GW included, not bolted on.
  • Modern interfaces — SBI adaptor for 5G-era interworking, IPsecProxy for secured access.
  • Supplementary Services AS — the services your subscribers actually notice, built in.
  • The IMS behind MCX and FRMCS — standards-compliant MCX requires an IMS, and several MCX vendors build theirs on our core.
HOW IT BUILDS

Engineering that pays off in OPEX
